These abbreviations are commonly used in soccer (football) data sets, often for betting and statistical analysis. Here is the long-form for each column, along with a brief explanation.

### Match Information
* **Div**: The league division.
* **Date**: The date of the match.
* **Time**: The kick-off time of the match.
* **HomeTeam**: The name of the home team.
* **AwayTeam**: The name of the away team.

---

### Full-Time Match Stats
* **FTHG**: Full-Time Home Team Goals. The number of goals scored by the home team at the end of the match.
* **FTAG**: Full-Time Away Team Goals. The number of goals scored by the away team at the end of the match.
* **FTR**: Full-Time Result. The final outcome of the match, where 'H' = Home Win, 'D' = Draw, 'A' = Away Win.

---

### Half-Time Match Stats
* **HTHG**: Half-Time Home Team Goals.
* **HTAG**: Half-Time Away Team Goals.
* **HTR**: Half-Time Result. 'H' = Home Win, 'D' = Draw, 'A' = Away Win at half-time.

---

### Match Incidents and Fouls
* **Referee**: The name of the match referee.
* **HS**: Home Team Shots.
* **AS**: Away Team Shots.
* **HST**: Home Team Shots on Target.
* **AST**: Away Team Shots on Target.
* **HF**: Home Team Fouls Committed.
* **AF**: Away Team Fouls Committed.
* **HC**: Home Team Corners.
* **AC**: Away Team Corners.
* **HY**: Home Team Yellow Cards.
* **AY**: Away Team Yellow Cards.
* **HR**: Home Team Red Cards.
* **AR**: Away Team Red Cards.

---

### Betting Odds (Pre-Match)
These columns represent the odds offered by different bookmakers before the match. The abbreviations for bookmakers include **B365** (Bet365), **BW** (Bwin), **IW** (Interwetten), **PS** (Pinnacle Sports), **WH** (William Hill), and **VC** (Victor Chandler).

* **B365H**: Bet365 Home Win Odds.
* **B365D**: Bet365 Draw Odds.
* **B365A**: Bet365 Away Win Odds.
* **MaxH**: Maximum Home Win Odds across all bookmakers.
* **MaxD**: Maximum Draw Odds.
* **MaxA**: Maximum Away Win Odds.
* **AvgH**: Average Home Win Odds across all bookmakers.
* **AvgD**: Average Draw Odds.
* **AvgA**: Average Away Win Odds.

---

### Goal Line and Asian Handicap Odds (Pre-Match)
* **B365>2.5**: Bet365 Over 2.5 Goals Odds (odds that the total goals will be more than 2.5).
* **B365<2.5**: Bet365 Under 2.5 Goals Odds (odds that the total goals will be less than 2.5).
* **P>2.5**: Pinnacle Sports Over 2.5 Goals Odds.
* **P<2.5**: Pinnacle Sports Under 2.5 Goals Odds.
* **Max>2.5**: Maximum Over 2.5 Goals Odds.
* **Max<2.5**: Maximum Under 2.5 Goals Odds.
* **Avg>2.5**: Average Over 2.5 Goals Odds.
* **Avg<2.5**: Average Under 2.5 Goals Odds.
* **AHh**: Asian Handicap. The handicap value for the match.
* **B365AHH**: Bet365 Asian Handicap Home. Odds for the home team with the given handicap.
* **B365AHA**: Bet365 Asian Handicap Away. Odds for the away team with the given handicap.
* **PAHH**: Pinnacle Sports Asian Handicap Home.
* **PAHA**: Pinnacle Sports Asian Handicap Away.
* **MaxAHH**: Maximum Asian Handicap Home Odds.
* **MaxAHA**: Maximum Asian Handicap Away Odds.
* **AvgAHH**: Average Asian Handicap Home Odds.
* **AvgAHA**: Average Asian Handicap Away Odds.

---

### Betting Exchange Odds
These columns relate to betting exchange data, where players bet against each other rather than a bookmaker. The abbreviations **BF** (Betfair) and **1XB** (1xBet) represent these platforms. **BF** odds often have a a back and lay price. Here, we see one side of that.
